Privacy Policy for Tabby

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Tabby ("we", "our", "the app") is a group expense-splitting app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (via Google Sign-In):
When you sign in with your Google account, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo. We use this solely to identify you within your groups and to display your name/photo to other group members you interact with.

Expense and group data you provide:
Group names, member lists, expenses, amounts, currencies, split configurations, comments, and settlement records you create or that other group members share with you. This data is stored to provide the app's core functionality (tracking who owes whom).

Push notification tokens:
If you enable notifications, we store a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver alerts about new expenses, comments, and settlements in your groups.

Crash and diagnostic data:
We use Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and performance diagnostics (e.g., device type, OS version, app version, stack traces) to identify and fix bugs. This data does not include your expense contents.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate core app features: creating groups, recording expenses, calculating balances, and settling debts between group members.
  • To send you notifications about activity in your groups.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes or performance issues.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Information Sharing

Your name, photo, and the expenses/balances you record are visible to other members of the same group(s) — this is necessary for the app's core purpose of shared expense tracking. We do not share your data with outside third parties except:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, push notifications, hosting) — as our infrastructure provider.
  • Sentry — for crash/error reporting.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account and group memberships remain active. If you delete a group or leave one, associated records may be retained in other members' group history since expenses are shared records. You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored in access-controlled cloud databases. Authentication uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 / Firebase Authentication with rotating tokens.

6. Children's Privacy

Tabby is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
