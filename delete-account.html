Delete Your Tabby Account

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Tabby account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to mail2nawaz2000@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you use to sign in to Tabby.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Tabby account.
  3. We will confirm your request by replying to that email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email address, profile photo).
  • Your push notification (device) tokens.
  • Your group memberships and your notification and activity history.

What is kept

  • Shared group records. Expenses, settlements and comments you added to groups shared with other people are kept so that the other members' balances stay accurate. After deletion, your name on these records is replaced with "Deleted user" and they are no longer linked to your email address.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports (Sentry) are not tied to your account profile and are automatically removed after 90 days.

Questions

For questions about your data, see our Privacy Policy or contact mail2nawaz2000@gmail.com.
