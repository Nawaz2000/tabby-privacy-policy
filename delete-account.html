Delete Your Tabby Account

Tabby app · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Tabby account (the Tabby expense-splitting app on Google Play) and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Tabby and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you have unsettled balances you will be shown them first and asked to type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion instead:

  1. Send an email to mail2nawaz2000@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you use to sign in to Tabby.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Tabby account.
  3. We will confirm your request by replying to that email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and profile photo.
  • Your sign-in details and login sessions.
  • Your push notification (device) tokens.
  • Your friend connections, group memberships, and your notification and activity history.

Retention period: none for the data above. It is removed at the time you delete in the app, or within 30 days of an email request. Copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.

What is kept

  • Shared group records. Expenses, settlements and comments you added to groups shared with other people are kept so that the other members' balances stay accurate. After deletion, your name on these records is replaced with "Deleted user" and they are no longer linked to your email address. These records are kept for as long as the group exists.
  • Unsettled balances. Deleting your account does not cancel amounts you owe or are owed. They remain on record for the other members of your groups, shown against "Deleted user". We recommend settling up first.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports (Sentry) are not tied to your account profile and are automatically removed after 90 days.
